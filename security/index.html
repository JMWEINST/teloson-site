Built so you do not have to trust us.

We never hold your money

Your account is at your broker, in your name. We cannot move money in or out of it.

We never see your keys

The installer stores them in your home folder. They talk to your broker and data services directly from your Mac.

Approve-first, in code

Orders on your real account only follow your click. Chat orders are switched off in code for every client.

It halts rather than improvises

Missing data, a crash, a limit reached: the desk stops, tells you why, and waits for you.

No public sign-up

Every account is made by hand. The installer refuses anyone without one, and repeated failed attempts are throttled.

A record you keep

Every decision is logged on your Mac, dated to the second. Export it and check our work.

Security questions.

Something not covered? Ask us.

What does Teloson's server see?
Your client code and email when you sign in on the client page and when the installer checks your account. It records that an install happened. It does not see your keys, your positions, your plans or your messages.
What does the reasoning model see?
The questions the seats ask it, sent with your own key: prices, headlines, your limits and holdings as needed to reason about a plan. Never your broker keys.
Could a bug place an order without me?
On a client desk the only code path to a real order is an approved plan, and limits are checked again before it is sent. The practice desk is the one place it trades on its own, and it only has paper money.
What if my Mac is lost?
Revoke the keys at your broker and data services, and tell us so we can revoke your client code. Your money is at your broker, not on the Mac.

A small number of clients at a time.

Tell us a little about you. We reply personally, usually within a few days. If it is a fit, the questionnaire comes next, and your account after that.
