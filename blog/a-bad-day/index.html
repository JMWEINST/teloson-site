What the floor does on a bad day

A bad-day halt, an emergency brake, and messages that repeat until you answer. A walk through a rough session, minute by minute.

JW
Jack WeinsteinFounder and CEO

Limits only matter on the days you need them. Here is what happens, in order, when a session goes badly.

Halfway to the brake

When the account falls halfway to the emergency brake, a heads-up goes out on every channel the client chose. Nothing halts yet.

The bad-day halt

When the day's loss reaches the bad-day limit, trading stops for the rest of the session. Nothing is sold. The message says why, and it repeats every two hours until the client acknowledges it. This halt cannot be released early; it waits for the next New York session.

The emergency brake

If the account falls to the brake, everything halts until the client releases it personally from the dashboard. Again, nothing is sold on their behalf.

The summary

At 17:05 New York time the daily summary goes out: the value, the day's move, what is waiting, and that the floor is halted. You can try the whole thing with your own limits on our home page.
