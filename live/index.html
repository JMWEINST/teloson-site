What you need

A Mac, the code from your welcome email, and about ten minutes. It asks for your own API keys as it goes. Blank is allowed for each.

What it does first

Starts the practice desk. Real prices, real news, fake money, for a couple of weeks. It trades that fake money on its own so you can watch its judgement.

What it never does

Send your keys anywhere. The desk listens only to your own computer. We cannot see it, and it never holds your money.

Teloson is not a public service. There is no sign-up. A desk is built after your questionnaire is signed and countersigned, and the account is created by hand. The installer checks your code and email against that account and refuses everyone else.
A day on the floor

Between the opening bell and your inbox.

Times are New York. The seats named here are real seats on the floor, each doing the one job it is built for. Scroll and the hours light up.

New York06:55Session opens
  1. 06:55
    TCTrading Calendar opens the session.

    Wire Freshness and Data Provenance check that overnight data is real, recent and from where it says it is. Anything stale is marked unknown, and unknown means no opinion.

  2. 09:31
    LTLive Tape starts reporting.

    Bid-Ask Spread, Quote Staleness and Market Breadth take their measurements every tick. The floor talks to itself. You can read along or ignore it.

  3. 10:12
    DCA plan is tabled.

    Enough seats converge on one name. All 74 review it, and Drawdown Control, Value at Risk and the Kill Switch hold the veto. No vetoes, so it passes arithmetically. Only then does anyone write up why.

  4. 10:12
    YOUIt lands in your queue.

    A message goes out on whatever you chose. The plan shows the size, the limit, the reasoning and 45:00 on the clock. Let it expire and it is gone. Revive it later and it gets re-priced and re-reviewed.

  5. 10:41
    ORYou approve.

    Preflight and Idempotency check the order, Order Routing sends it, Fill Quality reports what it actually cost. You get the fill on your phone inside the minute.

  6. 14:20
    DLA bad day, say.

    The book slips past your bad-day limit. Daily Limits halts the floor for the rest of the session and messages you, again every two hours until you acknowledge it. Nothing is sold. What you hold stays yours.

  7. 17:05
    PMThe summary.

    Account value, the day's move, what is waiting on you, whether anything is halted, and any limit that differs from the one you signed. Then the floor keeps watching, quietly, until tomorrow.

The rules it cannot break

You set the limits. The software cannot talk itself past them.

Each comes from your questionnaire and is enforced in code before any order reaches your broker. If something it needs to know is missing, it refuses rather than guesses.

Straight talk

A good-looking floor is not a good result.

This is experimental software. Its own validation harness has certified zero trading signals as statistically proven, and in its own out-of-sample backtests nothing it has tried beats buy-and-hold. It does not pretend otherwise, and neither do we.

Losses are possible at all times. Stops fill at the next available price, not the stop price, and overnight gaps have gone well past them. Teloson is software and setup. It is not investment advice, and it never holds your money.
