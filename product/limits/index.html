Risk limits

Every number comes from the questionnaire you signed. Each one is checked before any order reaches your broker, and the desk refuses rather than guesses.

Hard rules, not suggestions.

Checked before every order.

Preflight runs every limit against the plan and against what is already in flight. Pass them all and the plan reaches you. Fail one and it is rejected with the reason, and you hear about it.

preflight · plan 0418
10:12:27 checking 6 limits against plan 0418
✓ emergency brake −0.4% of 10%
✓ position cap 2.1% of 5% (with orders in flight)
✓ bad-day halt −0.4% of 3%
✓ losing streak 1 of 3
✓ daily orders 3 of 20
✓ daily dollars $5,240 of $25,000
→ 14 veto seats polled, 14 clear

Halts that only you release.

The bad-day halt stops trading for the rest of the session and waits for the next New York open. The emergency brake stops everything until you say otherwise. Nothing is sold on your behalf, and the message says why.
